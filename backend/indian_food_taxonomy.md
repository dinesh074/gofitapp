# Indian Food Taxonomy — North to South, All Regions

Reference checklist for expanding `indian_food_db.json` coverage and for
sourcing test/training photos. Organized by region → category. `V`/`NV` marks
vegetarian / non-vegetarian. This is a **coverage map**, not a nutrition
source — see `indian_food_db_expanded.json` for draft per-unit macros, and
verify against IFCT (Indian Food Composition Tables, National Institute of
Nutrition, Hyderabad) before trusting any number in production.

## SUGGESTED PRIORITIZATION FOR TRAINING/TESTING DATA COLLECTION

1. **Tier 1 (highest daily-use frequency, cover first)**: roti, rice, dal, sabzi/mixed veg, idli, dosa, sambar, curd, chai — already anchored, keep expanding variants.
2. **Tier 2 (very common, currently missing)**: pav bhaji, vada pav, poha, upma, paneer tikka, chicken curry variants, fish curry variants, biryani variants by region, thali (composite).
3. **Tier 3 (regional differentiators worth having for coverage claims)**: one signature dish per state capital region — this is what lets you credibly say "we cover India," not just North Indian restaurant food.
4. **Tier 4 (long tail)**: festival/occasion foods, hyper-regional dishes — lowest priority, address after 1–3 are solid.
