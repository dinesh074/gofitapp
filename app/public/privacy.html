Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · gofit.today

This describes what gofit.today ("we", "the app") actually collects and does with your information. We've written it to match the real behavior of the app, not generic boilerplate.

What we collect

  • Google account info — your name, email address, and profile picture, when you sign in with Google. We use this only to create and identify your account.
  • Food photos — when you scan a meal, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI to identify the food and estimate calories/macros. We don't keep a permanent copy of your photos on our servers after analysis.
  • Meal logs, weight history, and your profile (age, height, weight, goals, diet preference) — these are stored in our database, tied to your account, so your history follows you across devices when you sign in. A copy is also cached in your browser/device for fast, offline-friendly access.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — only whether a payment succeeded.
  • Community posts — anything you post to the community feed is visible to other signed-in users.
  • Feedback you submit — tied to your account so we can follow up if needed.
Cookies & local storage: on the web version, we use your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to keep you signed in and remember your preferences on that specific browser. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Who we share data with

  • Google — for sign-in, and for analyzing food photos (Gemini AI).
  • Razorpay — for processing Pro subscription payments.
  • Supabase / Render / Vercel — the infrastructure providers that host our database, backend, and website. They store data on our behalf; they don't use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time from your Profile.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us (below) — we'll remove your account row, sign-in tokens, and any posts/feedback tied to it.

Payments

Pro subscription payments are processed by Razorpay in Indian Rupees. Our server never sees your payment credentials — only Razorpay's confirmation that a payment succeeded.

Children

gofit.today isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
